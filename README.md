# Jacob Korsgaard

A collection of my work on empirical finance, asset pricing, financial econometrics, and statistical methods, helping me stay close to the topics I find most interesting.

This GitHub contains methodological libraries, empirical finance projects, and selected university projects.

---

## Libraries

Structured libraries combining theory, implementation, and empirical examples, with code for reproducible analysis.

- [Factor Asset Pricing Library](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/factor-asset-pricing-library)
  A structured library on empirical asset pricing, covering factor construction, portfolio formation, performance evaluation, asset pricing tests, momentum, and related topics.

- [Statistical Methods for Finance](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/finance-statistical-methods-library)
  A collection of statistical and econometric methods used in finance, including univariate and multivariate statistics, risk measurement, and time-series methods.

---

## Empirical Finance Projects

Standalone empirical projects, replications, and research implementations based on topics of particular interest to me.

- [Slow Minus Quick Factor](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/slow-minus-quick-factor)
  Implementation of the Slow Minus Quick (SMQ) factor developed in my master's thesis, studying firm adjustment frictions as a source of cross-sectional expected returns.

- [Volatility-Managed Momentum](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/volatility-managed-factors-and-momentum)
  Empirical analysis of volatility-managed factor strategies and factor momentum, with particular emphasis on why volatility management appears especially effective for momentum strategies.

- [Fama–French Five-Factor Replication](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/fama-french-five-factor-replication)
  Independent replication of the Fama–French five factors using CRSP and Compustat data, validated against the Kenneth French Data Library.

## University Projects

Selected projects completed as part of university coursework.

- [Bayesian Econometrics — Ca' Foscari](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/bayesian-econometrics-ca-foscari)
  Coursework and implementations in Bayesian econometrics and related applications.

- [Applied Statistical Learning in R](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/applied-statistical-learning-r)
  Statistical learning methods implemented in R, including regression, classification, regularization, clustering, and PCA.

- [TSLA Daily Difference](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/TSLA-daily-difference)
  Time-series analysis of TSLA’s daily difference, defined as the difference between the maximum and minimum values within a trading day and interpreted as a measure of daily uncertainty.
