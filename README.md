# Jacob Korsgaard

I work with empirical finance, asset pricing, financial econometrics, and statistical methods.

This GitHub contains methodological libraries, empirical asset pricing projects, and selected university projects.

## Libraries

The libraries are intended as structured references for methods and concepts I use in quantitative finance and empirical research. They combine theoretical notes, implementation, and empirical examples.

### [Factor Asset Pricing Library](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/factor-asset-pricing-library)
A structured library on empirical asset pricing, covering factor construction, portfolio formation, performance evaluation, asset pricing tests, and extensions such as momentum and factor momentum.

### [Statistical Methods for Finance](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/finance-statistical-methods-library)
A library of statistical and econometric methods used in finance, covering univariate and multivariate statistics, risk measurement, and time-series methods.

## Empirical Asset Pricing & Quantitative Finance Projects

Standalone projects applying empirical finance methods to specific research questions, replications, and extensions.

### [Slow Minus Quick Factor](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/slow-minus-quick-factor)
Implementation of the Slow Minus Quick factor developed in my master's thesis, studying firm-level adjustment frictions and their relation to expected returns.

### [Volatility-Managed Momentum](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/volatility-managed-momentum)
Empirical analysis of volatility-managed momentum strategies, including the relation between momentum returns, volatility, and dynamic portfolio scaling.

### [Fama–French Five-Factor Replication](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/fama-french-five-factor-replication)
Replication of the Fama–French five factors and standard portfolio sorts using CRSP and Compustat data, with validation against the Kenneth French Data Library.

## University Projects

Selected projects completed as part of university coursework.

### [Bayesian Econometrics — Ca' Foscari](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/bayesian-econometrics-ca-foscari)
Coursework and implementations from Bayesian econometrics, including Bayesian inference and applications in economics and finance.

### [Applied Statistical Learning in R](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/applied-statistical-learning-r)
Projects and exercises covering statistical learning methods implemented in R.

### [TSLA Daily Difference](https://github.com/jacobkorsgaard/TSLA-daily-difference)
A university project examining the statistical properties of daily Tesla stock-price changes and related time-series methods.
