Jacob Korsgaard

A collection of my work on empirical finance, asset pricing, financial econometrics, and statistical methods, helping me stay close to my interests.

This GitHub contains methodological libraries, empirical asset pricing projects, and selected university projects.

Libraries

Structured libraries combining theory, implementation, and empirical examples.

Factor Asset Pricing Library
A structured library on empirical asset pricing, covering factor construction, portfolio formation, performance evaluation, asset pricing tests, momentum, and related topics.

Statistical Methods for Finance
A collection of statistical and econometric methods used in finance, including univariate and multivariate statistics, risk measurement, and time-series methods.

Empirical Finance Projects

Standalone empirical projects, replications, and research implementations.

Slow Minus Quick Factor
Implementation of the Slow Minus Quick factor developed in my master's thesis, studying firm adjustment frictions and expected returns.

Volatility-Managed Momentum
Empirical analysis of momentum, volatility, and volatility-managed portfolio strategies.

Fama–French Five-Factor Replication
Independent replication of the Fama–French five factors using CRSP and Compustat data, validated against the Kenneth French Data Library.

University Projects

Selected projects completed as part of university coursework.

Bayesian Econometrics — Ca' Foscari
Coursework and implementations in Bayesian econometrics and related applications.

Applied Statistical Learning in R
Statistical learning methods implemented in R, including regression, classification, regularization, clustering, and PCA.

TSLA Daily Difference
Time-series analysis of daily Tesla price changes.

Areas of Interest

Empirical asset pricing, financial econometrics, factor models, return predictability, portfolio construction, Bayesian econometrics, and statistical learning.
